# Password Reset Guide (Fictional company: Acme Corp)

## Reset your work password
1. Go to selfservice.acme.example.
2. Click "Forgot password" and enter your work email.
3. Approve the verification code sent to your registered phone.
4. Choose a new password.

## Password rules
- At least 12 characters.
- Must include a number and a symbol.
- Cannot reuse your last 5 passwords.
- Expires every 90 days.

## Locked account
After 5 wrong attempts the account locks for 30 minutes. If you cannot wait, contact the helpdesk with your employee ID. The helpdesk will never ask for your password.
