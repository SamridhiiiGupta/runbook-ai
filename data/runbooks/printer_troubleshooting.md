# Printer Troubleshooting Guide (Fictional company: Acme Corp)

## Printer not found
1. Check the printer is on and shows "Ready".
2. Make sure your laptop is on the office Wi-Fi, not a guest network.
3. Open Settings, Printers, and click "Add printer".

## Paper jam
Turn the printer off, open the rear tray, pull the paper out slowly in the direction of travel, and close the tray. Never use sharp tools.

## Print queue stuck
Open the print queue, cancel all documents, then restart the "Print Spooler" service or restart your laptop.
