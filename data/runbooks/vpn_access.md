# VPN Access Guide (Fictional company: Acme Corp)

Use the VPN to reach internal tools from home.

## Install the VPN client
1. Open the Software Portal at portal.acme.example.
2. Download "AcmeConnect" for your operating system.
3. Run the installer and restart your laptop.

## Connect to the VPN
1. Open AcmeConnect and enter the server vpn.acme.example.
2. Sign in with your work email and approve the code on your phone.
3. Wait for the status to show "Connected".

## VPN troubleshooting
- Error 809: your home router is blocking the VPN. Try a mobile hotspot.
- Stuck on "Connecting": close AcmeConnect, restart it, and try again.
- Still failing after two attempts: create a ticket with category "Network".
